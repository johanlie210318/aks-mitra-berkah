How the two are contracted

PT AKS Mitra Berkah and Zestha Vutura are separate companies under common ownership. Our own IT registration covers support, installation, and recovery; it does not cover systems consulting or software development, and we do not sign for those. Where a job needs them, Zestha contracts that part directly, and you will see two agreements rather than one.

Structured cabling and electrical installation are separate registrations again, and are subcontracted.

Supply & support

The part contracted directly with PT AKS Mitra Berkah, on the same terms as the building work: written scope, stated exclusions, one party answering the phone.

Registered scope: 62900 · 46100 · 46901 · 46902

Procurement
Specification, quotation, and delivery of workstations, laptops, printers, and peripherals. Supplier invoices open to you.
Deployment
Machines configured and licensed, accounts and shared drives created, data migrated from old equipment, old drives wiped and certified.
Ongoing support
Fault diagnosis and repair, on a call-out or a monthly basis. Response times fixed in the agreement rather than promised in conversation.
Backup & recovery
Backups configured and — the part usually skipped — restored from, on a schedule, to prove they work. Recovery from failed drives where the media allows it.

Not included: systems consulting, network or systems design, software development. Those are separate registrations we do not hold.
