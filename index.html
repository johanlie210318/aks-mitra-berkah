01 · How we are set up

Three stages. One party carrying them.

Our registered scope covers planning, wholesale supply, and construction. That combination is unusual for a company this size, and it is the whole reason to hire us.

STAGE 01

Plan

Scope written out, quantities taken off, budget tested against real material prices — before anyone commits.

STAGE 02

Supply

Materials bought on our own wholesale registration and delivered to site, with the invoices open to you.

STAGE 03

Build

Residential building work and specialist trades executed and supervised against the scope we wrote in stage one.

02 · Capabilities

What we are set up to take on

Residential construction

New-build houses, extensions, and full renovations of occupied homes — including the sequencing that keeps a family living in the building while work runs.

Registered scope: 41011

Specialist works

Discrete packages taken on for owners or main contractors: structural repair, waterproofing, finishing trades, and coordination of specialist subcontractors.

Registered scope: 43400, 43909

Material supply

Procurement and delivery of building materials, either bundled into a build contract or supplied on its own to owners running their own crew.

Registered scope: 46100, 46901, 46902

Pre-construction advice

Owners who are not ready to build yet can engage us for the planning stage alone — feasibility, buildability review, quantities, and a defensible budget. If you then take the work to another contractor, the documents are yours. Registered scope: 70209

03 · Why work with us

Four commitments, fixed in writing

We are a young company. These are things we can promise on day one and be held to — not claims about a past you cannot check.

01

The director signs and runs the job

One decision-maker, reachable directly. You will not be handed to someone you never met after the contract is signed.

02

Scope and exclusions before price

What is in, what is out, and what triggers a variation — agreed on paper first. Most disputes on small builds are really disputes about what was excluded.

03

Materials at our own trade terms

Wholesale trade sits inside our registered scope, so material margin is a line you can see and question rather than something buried in a lump sum.

04

Permit status stated up front

For work that needs a licence, certification, or permit we do not yet hold, we say so before quoting and tell you who does hold it.
